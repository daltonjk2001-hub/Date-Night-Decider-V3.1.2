Privacy Policy

Date Night Decider · Version 3.1.2 · Effective October 2026
🔒 Local-First Privacy Guarantee: Date Night Decider is engineered to respect your privacy completely. All date selections, scrapbook ratings, custom cards, and settings remain stored strictly on your local device.

1. Data Storage & Ownership

Your date history, completion ratings, and customized preferences are saved locally using IndexedDB and LocalStorage in your browser or native app container. We do not maintain central servers that store your personal date history.

2. Peer-to-Peer Live Sync

When using the Live Couple Room feature, real-time swiping is established directly between your device and your partner's device using encrypted WebRTC peer-to-peer technology (via PeerJS). No swipe choices or room messages are logged or retained on intermediary signaling servers.

3. In-App Purchases & Billing

Expansion pack unlocks are processed through standard App Store or Google Play store billing mechanisms. We do not collect, process, or store financial credentials, credit cards, or payment details.

4. Analytics & Tracking

Date Night Decider contains zero third-party advertising frameworks, zero behavioral tracking scripts, and zero telemetry SDKs. We do not track your location or sell personal data to third parties.

5. Your Data Control

You have 100% control over your data. You can export a full JSON backup of your scrapbook and settings, or wipe all application data instantly from the app's Settings panel at any time.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding privacy or data handling, please contact us at support@datenightdecider.app.
