Frequently Asked Questions

How does Matcher mode work?

Both partners review date cards by swiping right to like or left to pass. When both partners like the same date idea, it becomes a mutual match! If multiple dates match, you enter a fun 1-on-1 elimination draft to pick the winner.

How does Live Room synchronization work?

Tap 'Host Live Room' in Settings to generate a private room code or shareable invite link. Have your partner tap 'Join Live Room' or open the link on their device. You'll both see the same cards synchronized in real-time!

Can I restore purchased expansion packs?

Yes! In Settings under Expansion Store or Tools, tap 'Restore Purchases' to automatically restore any previously purchased expansion packs on your device.

Can I use Date Night Decider offline?

Absolutely. Date Night Decider is a Progressive Web App (PWA) with full offline support. All built-in ideas, custom decks, and local scrapbook features work without an internet connection.
